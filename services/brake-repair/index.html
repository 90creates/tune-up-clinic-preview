FAQ

Brake Repair: questions, answered

Have a question we didn’t cover? Call (909) 792-2522.

How do I know if I need new brakes?

Squealing usually means worn pads; grinding means come in right away. A soft or pulsing pedal, pulling to one side, or a brake warning light are all reasons to have them checked.

Do I need new rotors or just pads?

It depends on how worn and how smooth the rotors are. We measure them and show you before recommending anything.

How much does brake repair cost?

It depends on your vehicle and what’s worn. We inspect first and give you a clear price before any work starts.

Can I walk in for a brake inspection?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome seven days a week. Call ahead if you want to check the wait.
