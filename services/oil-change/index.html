FAQ

Oil Change: questions, answered

Have a question we didn’t cover? Call (909) 792-2522.

Do I need an appointment for an oil change?

No. Walk-ins are welcome during open hours, seven days a week. Many regulars never make an appointment.

How long does an oil change take?

Most oil changes are quick. Customers regularly tell us they’re in and out in under half an hour. Busy days can take a little longer.

How often should I change my oil?

Follow your car’s maintenance schedule or oil-life monitor. Cars that use full synthetic oil typically go longer between changes than older cars on conventional oil.

Will you try to upsell me?

No. “No upselling” is one of the things our customers mention most. We’ll show you what we see and tell you what can wait.

Are you open on weekends?

Yes. We’re open Saturday and Sunday from 8 AM to 3 PM, and weekdays from 7:30 AM to 4:30 PM.
