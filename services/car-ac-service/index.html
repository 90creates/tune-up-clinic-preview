FAQ

Car A/C Service: questions, answered

Have a question we didn’t cover? Call (909) 792-2522.

Why is my car A/C blowing warm air?

Usually a refrigerant leak, a failing compressor or condenser, an electrical problem or a stuck blend door. We diagnose before recommending a repair.

Can’t I just recharge it?

Only if there’s no leak. Otherwise the refrigerant escapes again. We check first.

Why does my A/C smell musty?

Often a dirty cabin air filter. Replacing it usually helps.

Can you check my radiator too?

Yes. Radiator and cooling-system service is one of our regular services, and it’s smart to check before summer.
